Postdoctoral Researcher at [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://www.epfl.ch/) in the chair of High Performance Numerical Algorithms and Simulations [(HPNALGS)](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/hpnalgs/), supervised by [Prof. Laura Grigori](https://people.epfl.ch/laura.grigori).

Before that Ph.D in applied mathematics at [Ecole Centrale de Nantes](https://www.ec-nantes.fr/) supervised by [Prof. Anthony Nouy](https://anthony-nouy.github.io/).
Graduate engineer from Ecole Centrale de Nantes, specializing in applied mathematics and research.

My research topics mainly revolve around dimension reduction and randomized methods, especially in a context of high dimensional function approximation, and inverse problems.
I try my best to promote open science, both through my research work and by disseminating scientific culture to the public.
